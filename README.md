# LOKY 🇪🇬

### All Egyptian Local Brands. One Place.

LOKY is a mobile marketplace concept that brings Egyptian local brands together in one platform, making it easier for shoppers to discover products, compare prices, and shop from multiple brands in one place.

## 📸 Project Preview

![LOKY Marketplace Preview](screenshots/loky-preview.png)

## 🎯 About the Project

Shopping from Egyptian local brands often requires browsing multiple Instagram pages, websites, and separate online stores. This can make product discovery, price comparison, and checkout more complicated.

LOKY aims to solve this problem by creating a unified marketplace where customers can explore different local brands and purchase products through a single shopping experience.

## ✨ Features

* 🛍️ **Multi-Brand Catalog** — Browse products from different Egyptian brands.
* 🔎 **Search & Filters** — Find products by category, brand, or price.
* ⚖️ **Product Comparison** — Compare products and prices before purchasing.
* 🛒 **Unified Shopping Cart** — Add products from multiple brands to one cart.
* 🔒 **Secure Checkout Concept** — A convenient and straightforward checkout experience.
* 📦 **Order Tracking** — Track orders from confirmation to delivery.
* ❤️ **Favorites** — Save favorite brands and products.
* ✨ **Personalized Recommendations** — Discover products based on shopping preferences.

## 💰 Business Model

LOKY explores four potential revenue streams:

1. **Commission per Order** — A percentage of each completed order.
2. **Seller Subscriptions** — Premium tools and plans for local brands.
3. **Premium Membership** — Exclusive discounts and customer benefits.
4. **Delivery Fees** — Delivery charges applied to orders.

## 🎨 Design System

The visual direction focuses on a clean, modern, and welcoming shopping experience.

* **Style:** Minimal and elegant.
* **Colors:** Warm neutrals with soft pastel accents.
* **Components:** Rounded cards, subtle shadows, and simple icons.
* **Typography:** Elegant serif headings paired with readable sans-serif body text.

## 🎯 Project Goals

* Make Egyptian local brands easier to discover.
* Simplify shopping across multiple brands.
* Help users compare products and prices.
* Support independent Egyptian businesses.
* Create a consistent and convenient online shopping experience.

## 🚀 Vision

To become a go-to destination for discovering and shopping from Egyptian local brands, connecting independent businesses with customers through one accessible marketplace.
